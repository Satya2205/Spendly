Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language — please give it a read so you know exactly where you stand.

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect the information you give us — typically your name, email address, and a password. While you use the service, we store the expense records you choose to enter: amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions or notes you add.

We also automatically receive basic technical information such as your browser type, device, IP address, and timestamps of when you sign in. We use this only to keep the service running smoothly and to spot unusual activity.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect to operate Spendly — to display your expense history, generate category and monthly summaries, authenticate you on each visit, and keep your records safe with backups. We also use it to send you important service notices, such as password resets or policy updates.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your expense data to target you with advertising. Spendly is a tracking tool, not an ad network.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers and transmitted over encrypted connections. Passwords are hashed — we cannot see or recover them — and access to your account requires your login credentials. We run regular backups so that your records can be restored if something goes wrong on our end.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection against every threat. If we ever become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you promptly and walk you through what happened and what to do next.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of trusted third parties to operate — for example, a hosting provider that runs our servers and an email service that delivers password reset messages. These providers only receive the information they need to perform their role and are contractually obligated to protect it.

We do not embed third-party advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or social widgets in the app. If we ever add optional integrations in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can review and update your account information at any time from your profile page. You can delete individual expense records, and you can delete your entire account — along with all of the data associated with it — from the account settings.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data or ask us to restrict how we process it. To exercise any of these rights, just reach out using the contact details below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If a change is material — for example, if we begin collecting a new category of data — we will let you know through the app or by email before the change takes effect.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please write to us at support@spendly.app. We read every message and will do our best to help.
